What goes over the wire

The browser builds this request for you. The boundary string separates the parts.

POST /succesPost HTTP/1.1
Host: localhost:4442
Content-Type: multipart/form-data; boundary=----X9f2
Content-Length: 48213

------X9f2
Content-Disposition: form-data; name="binary"; filename="photo.png"
Content-Type: image/png

<48 000 raw bytes>
------X9f2--

The route's uploadPath decides the destination folder, and bodySize decides how big a body is accepted. Over the limit the server answers 413.
