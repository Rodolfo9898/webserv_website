Stability

Throw requests at it. It stays up.

The subject demands a server that never crashes and never hangs. Fire a burst of requests from your browser at the /siege route and watch throughput, latency and status codes live.

A browser opens only about six connections per server at a time, so the effective parallelism here is lower than the slider. It is a friendly smoke test, not a benchmark.

For a real load test, point siege at the route made for it: 
siege -b -c25 -t10S http://localhost:4442/siege
